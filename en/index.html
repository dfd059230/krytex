FAQ

What is the minimum order?
It depends on the format: studios start from one case; dealer and distributor terms are calculated individually per region and volume. Send a request and we will share pricing and terms.
How fast is shipping?
Local production and warehousing in China allow us to ship bestsellers within days. Lead times for made-to-order items are confirmed by your manager.
Can I get samples?
Yes, product samples are available to potential partners for testing. List the formulas you are interested in within your request.
Who handles documents and customs?
We provide the full document package for import and legal sales in China and assist partners at every step.
Do you train technicians?
Yes — hands-on training, video courses in Chinese and KRYTEX Academy certification are part of the partner program.
Is regional exclusivity possible?
Yes, distributors get territory protection up to provincial exclusivity — terms depend on committed purchase volumes.
